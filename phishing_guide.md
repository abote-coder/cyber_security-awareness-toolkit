# Phishing Awareness & Social Engineering Defense Guide
## What is Phishing?
Phishing is a social engineering technique where attackers trick individuals into revealing sensitive information—such as login credentials, credit card numbers, or personal identifying data—by posing as trustworthy entities.
## Key Phishing Indicators (Red Flags)
1. **Urgency and Threats:** Messages claiming your account will be suspended immediately if you do not act now.
2. **Generic Greetings:** Emails addressed as "Dear Customer" or "Dear Student" rather than your actual name.
3. **Mismatched URLs:** Hovering over links reveals web addresses that do not match the official organization domain.
4. **Suspicious Attachments:** Unexpected emails containing attachments such as `.exe`, `.zip`, `.xlsm`, or unexpected PDF files.
5. **Unusual Requests:** IT Helpdesk asking for your password, PIN, or multi-factor authentication approval code.
## Immediate Response Protocol
If you click a suspicious link or receive a phishing message:
1. **Disconnect:** Disconnect your device from Wi-Fi or Ethernet immediately.
2. **Report:** Forward the suspicious message to `report-phishing@campus-cybershield.edu`.
3. **Reset Credentials:** Change your account password immediately from an uncompromised device.
4. **Notify SOC:** Inform your Security Operations Center to initiate monitoring.
